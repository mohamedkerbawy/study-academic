# Research Project

Title of Research: **Optical Fiber Communication for High-Speed Computer Networks**

## Overview

The research explores the role of optical fiber technology in high-speed computer networks. It examines the principles of optical fiber communication, evaluates transmission performance, and analyzes the advantages of optical fiber over traditional copper-based communication media.

The study focuses on the factors that affect transmission quality, including attenuation, dispersion, bandwidth, and transmission distance. It also discusses the application of optical fiber technology in Local Area Networks (LANs), Metropolitan Area Networks (MANs), Wide Area Networks (WANs), and backbone network infrastructures.

## Research Objectives

- Study the fundamentals of optical fiber communication.
- Understand the role of optical fibers in computer networks.
- Analyze factors affecting optical transmission performance.
- Compare optical fiber and copper transmission media.
- Evaluate the advantages and limitations of optical fiber technology.
- Review the applications of optical fiber in modern network infrastructures.
